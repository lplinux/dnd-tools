# 🧪 Homebrew

Your own content, kept in one library: write a spell or a creature once, use it in any campaign.
Your Item Cards library is here too, as the *Items* tab.

**Access:** DM only — `/homebrew` (**Homebrew** among the DM tools on the home page).

## Features

- **A library, not per campaign.** An entry belongs to you, not to a campaign, so one spell used
  at two tables is one spell — edit it once and both have the change.
- **Spells**, with everything the stat sheet shows for an official one: level, school, casting
  time, range, components and material, duration, concentration, ritual, the text, what a
  higher-level slot does, and which classes have it.
- **Start from an official spell.** Pick an SRD spell when creating one and every field is filled
  in; change what you like. The entry remembers which spell it was based on.
- **Status** — *Draft* (being written), *Playtest* (in use, still being balanced) or *Final*.
- **Balance notes**, for you only: why it is the way it is, what to watch for at the table.
- **Per campaign: use, then reveal.** The *Campaigns* panel says which campaigns use an entry and
  which have revealed it to their players. Players only ever receive revealed entries, filtered
  on the server, and never your balance notes.

## Usage

Open **Homebrew**, press **＋ New**, and either fill the form in or start from an SRD spell. Pick
the spell on the left to see it as its card, with **✎ Edit** and **✕** beside its name.

Under **Campaigns**, tick **Uses it** for each campaign that should have it, and **Revealed** once
the party has learned it. Unticking *Uses it* takes it out of that campaign (and un-reveals it).

Deleting an entry takes it out of every campaign that used it; the confirmation names them.

## Creatures

A creature is a **stat block** — the same sheet an NPC's stat block and a character's stats use,
with the SRD monster import inside it. **＋ New** asks for a name, status and notes; then fill in
the sheet on the right and press its **💾 Save**. **✎ Details** changes the name, status and
balance notes.

An NPC can **start from one of your creatures**: in Manage Campaigns → NPCs → ⚔, pick it from
*Start from one of your creatures*. It is a **copy**, renamed to the NPC, so the two can differ
afterwards; if the NPC already has a stat block you are asked first.

## Items

The **Items** tab is your Item Cards library — the same cards as the Item Cards page, with its own
export and import. The public Item Cards page stays as the standalone tool for anyone not logged
in.

## On character sheets

A campaign's homebrew spells appear in the **stat sheet's spell pickers**, at their own level and
marked *homebrew*, both on a player's character sheet and on an NPC's stat block. In the spell
list a homebrew spell opens its own card. **Players are offered revealed spells only**; you see
every spell the campaign uses.

The sheet stores spell **names**. Where a homebrew spell and an SRD spell share a name, the sheet
means the homebrew one — the picker offers that name once, and the card is yours.

## Export / import

**⬇ Export** saves your whole library — spells and creatures — as one file: no ids, no campaigns, your
balance notes included (it is your file). **⬆ Import** adds the entries that are new to you and
skips any whose name you already have, naming them, so nothing you edited is replaced and
importing the same file twice changes nothing.

Homebrew is **not** part of a campaign file. A character's sheet carries only the spell's name,
so a campaign moved to another table keeps the names; where that table has no such homebrew, the
name is shown with no card behind it.
